1. Verification Proof

To reclaim any item, students must produce their active Chandigarh University Student Smart ID card and demonstrate specific knowledge of the item.

2. Digital Gate Pass

Once security approves your claim, a digital handover receipt is generated. Present this receipt at the custody desk to sign the release register.

3. 60-Day Retention Limit

Unclaimed items are retained in secure lockers for 60 calendar days before being transferred to student welfare charity drives or legal authorities.
